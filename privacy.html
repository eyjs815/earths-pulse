Earth's Pulse

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy covers "Earths Pulse Uploader" (the "App"), a personal tool used by the owner of the Earth's Pulse YouTube channel to upload videos to that channel. The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the App accesses

The App requests a single Google OAuth permission, youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails to the signed-in user's own YouTube channel. It does not read, collect or store your YouTube viewing history, comments, subscribers, contacts, email, or any other personal data.

How data is used and stored

The OAuth access token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer, is used only to upload that channel's videos, and is never sold, shared with, or sent to any third party other than Google/YouTube. The App has no servers, no analytics and no advertising, and uses no cookies.

Viewers

People who watch Earth's Pulse videos never interact with the App. Their data is handled by YouTube under the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can revoke the App's access at any time from your Google Account's security settings: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the stored token from the owner's computer also ends access.
